💲 FirstCry rate fix — correct a wrong MRP / rate on the PO, per FCID (applies to every FirstCry PO)
Use this when the PO prints the wrong MRP or price (the debit-note case). Enter the correct MRP and the correct rate incl. GST (the PO's “Landing Rate”). Base cost is worked out from the GST % on the PO. Quantity is optional: leave it empty to keep the PO quantity.

Purplle ASN filler: portal Excel in, upload-ready file out
On the Purplle portal open PO → select PO → Get ASN and download the SKU_Data Excel. Import it here, then import the invoice PDF. Invoice no, ship date, quantities and supplier codes fill from the invoice, and the invoice is checked against the PO. Check the table, then download and upload at ASN → Add New → Upload Excel. Upload the invoice PDF in the ASN Upload tab. Purplle's own formatting is kept.
Bulk ASN + Appointment: many POs, one drop, one download
Drop everything at once: the SKU_Data Excel files (one per PO), the tax invoice PDFs and, if you like, the Purplle PO PDFs. Files are sorted automatically and matched by PO number. Review the checklist, add appointment dates, then download all ASN files (and invoices) in one ZIP. The single-PO filler above is unchanged and shares the same files.
