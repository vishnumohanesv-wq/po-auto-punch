PO Punch turns a buyer's purchase-order PDF into rows you can paste into EasyEcom, a one-line entry for your dispatch tracker, and a checked invoice. It reads Purplle, FirstCry, FirstClub, Reliance Retail and Tata 1MG POs, and everything runs inside your browser.

        

          
1Upload the PO
Drop the buyer's PDF on the Convert page. The buyer is detected for you. Pick it by hand only if it guesses wrong.

          
2Check the numbers
Items, quantity and value are shown under the upload box. They should match the PDF before you go on.

          
3Copy and paste
Copy the SKU, Qty and Price rows into EasyEcom, and the one-line row into your Master Dispatch Tracker.

          
4Check the invoice
Before sending, compare the invoice with its PO on the Invoices page. Mismatches are listed in plain words.

Compare a PO with its invoice

        
  1. Open Invoices and choose Compare PO vs Invoice.
  2. Select the PO PDF and its invoice PDF together, in any order. Several pairs are fine.
  3. Read the verdict: Ready to send, Review or Fix, then the list of what differs.
  4. Use Copy report to paste the findings into WhatsApp or email, or export the report to Excel.

        
What is checked
  • Every invoice line exists on the PO
  • Quantity per line: excess, short, or not invoiced
  • Rate and MRP, GST rate and tax type (IGST or CGST + SGST)
  • Seller and buyer GSTIN, place of supply
  • Invoice date against the PO date and expiry
  • The invoice's own arithmetic and totals

        
Fill the Purplle ASN

        
  1. On the Purplle portal open PO, select the PO, Get ASN and download SKU_Data.xlsx.
  2. Here, import it with Import SKU_Data Excel. Several files are fine.
  3. Import the Purplle tax invoice PDF. Invoice number, ship date and quantities fill in and are checked against the PO.
  4. Review the table, then download the filled file.
  5. On the portal go to ASN, Add New, Upload Excel, upload the file and press Save. Upload the invoice PDF in the ASN Upload tab.

  • Nothing is uploaded. PO, invoice, inventory and ASN files are read inside your browser and never sent anywhere.
  • What is saved, and where. History, SKU mappings, settings, your workspace and your chosen look are stored in this browser only.

The totals do not match the PDF
Check the status line under the upload box. A difference usually means an unmapped code, a different Price source (pick the one that matches the PDF), or duplicate SKUs merged. Always compare with the PDF before punching.

          
A code shows as unmapped
Add it once under Sheet & Mapping, SKU mapping: the buyer's code and your SKU. It is remembered for every future PO.

          
Can I use it for my own company?
Yes. Open Sheet & Mapping, Workspace and set your company name, zones and warehouses. Then add your SKU mappings. The PDF readers are built for the four buyers listed under Buyers.

          
Can I use it on another computer or on my phone?
Yes, open the same link. Data stays in each browser, so use Back up and Restore under Sheet & Mapping, Pro tools to carry your mappings and history across.

          
Does it work without internet?
It needs a connection the first time, to load the PDF and Excel libraries. Your files are never uploaded.

          
A buyer changed its PO layout
Always compare the extracted values with the PDF. If a layout stops reading correctly, tell whoever maintains your copy of the tool so the reader can be updated.

          
How do I send rows to my Google Sheet?
Under Sheet & Mapping, Google Sheet link, paste your Apps Script web-app URL and key once. POs already in the sheet are skipped.

          
How do I change how it looks?
Press S for 42 looks, or use Theme for Auto, Light or Dark.

          
I deleted something by mistake
If you made a backup, restore it under Sheet & Mapping, Pro tools. Purplle POs you deleted from the batch list can be brought back with Restore deleted POs.
